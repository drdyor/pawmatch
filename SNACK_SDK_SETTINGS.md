# 📱 How to Check/Change SDK in Expo Snack

---

## 🔍 **WHERE TO FIND SDK SETTING:**

### **Option 1: Bottom of Screen**
- **Look at the bottom** of Expo Snack editor
- Should show: **"SDK: 50"** or similar
- If you see it, click to change

### **Option 2: Settings Menu**
- **Top right:** Click **"⚙️ Settings"** or **gear icon**
- Look for **"SDK Version"** or **"Expo SDK"**
- Change dropdown to **SDK 50** or **SDK 51**

### **Option 3: Package.json**
- **Left sidebar:** Find `package.json`
- Look for: `"expo": "~50.0.0"`
- Change to: `"expo": "~51.0.0"` (if Snack allows)

### **Option 4: Project Settings**
- **Top menu:** Look for **"Project"** or **"Config"**
- Should show SDK version there

---

## ✅ **WHAT TO DO:**

1. **Find SDK setting** (one of the above)
2. **Ensure it's SDK 50 or 51** (your Expo Go supports 54, so 50+ should work)
3. **Save** in Snack
4. **Get new QR code**
5. **Scan again**

---

## 🆘 **IF YOU CAN'T FIND IT:**

**Try this:**
1. **Save your Snack** (any changes)
2. **Close and reopen Snack**
3. **Look for SDK version** in bottom right
4. **Or check if there's a warning** about SDK mismatch
